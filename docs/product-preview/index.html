Home23

Your home, on iPhone and iPad

Talk, work and return to your shared history from Home23. Your Mac keeps the home running in the background.

Getting started

  1. Install Home23
  2. Connect iPhone and iPad
  3. Use your home
  4. Update Home23
  5. Recover and back up

1. Install Home23

Install the Home23 app on your Mac. It includes the background home manager and the software your home needs; they update together.

For a new home, choose Set up or open a home, enter your home and resident details, and connect your model provider. Anthropic and OpenAI account sign-in, supported API providers and local models are available choices. Follow the preparation status, then create and start your home.

If you already have a home, open or connect to it. Keep its residents, conversations, files and existing model settings. Bringing an older installation onto this release is a separate prepared transfer; creating a new home does not transfer your history.

In Home23 Settings, turn on Run my home at login and approve it in macOS Login Items if asked. The home manager then starts behind the scenes. A home you explicitly stopped stays stopped.

This release targets Apple silicon Macs with macOS 27 and iPhone/iPad with iOS/iPadOS 27. Keep the Mac awake and connected when you want your home available.

2. Connect iPhone and iPad

On the Mac, open Connect a device in the home manager. Follow the private connection setup, then copy or share the verified home address. Open Home23 on each iPhone or iPad, enter that address and finish pairing.

Each device connects to the same home. Existing paired devices keep their saved identity when the app is updated. Your model-provider credentials remain with the home.

3. Use your home

Use Talk for conversations, Work for ongoing tasks, and Library for your home’s material. Your Mac can stay in the background while you use iPhone or iPad. You can also use the Mac client.

On the Mac, Manage this home and backups… in Settings opens setup, account and backup controls when you need them. Open Dashboard opens the home’s private web dashboard.

4. Update Home23

On iPhone, iPad or Mac, open Settings → Home → Check for updates. Review the available version and choose Update Home23. Progress remains attached to the same update if your app disconnects or closes.

If Home23 says it is reconnecting, wait for the home’s status to return. Use Refresh status to check again. If an interrupted update offers Resume update, use it to continue.

When this device needs a newer app, Get Home23 app update opens its supported installation route. Other compatible family devices can update later. A missing release connection is shown as unavailable; it does not mean your software is current.

5. Recover and back up

Follow the update’s recovery message if it needs attention. If local recovery is required, open Manage this home and backups… on the Mac. Backups retain your existing home and can be inspected before recovery starts it. Keep the backup and its recovery key safe.

Some continuing homes depend on external folders or integrations. Recovery identifies those connections so missing material is handled before the home resumes.
