Home23 beta support

Help for using Home23 on iPhone and iPad with the home running on your Mac.

Install and connect

Use the private TestFlight invitation you received to install Home23. On the Mac running your home, open Connect a device in the home manager. Enter its verified address in the iPhone or iPad app and finish pairing. Keep the Mac awake and connected when you want the home available.

If you already use Home23, update the installed app without deleting it. Keep its saved connection and local pending messages.

If the home is unavailable

Check that the Mac and home are running and that your device can reach the home address. If Home23 shows a reconnecting state, wait for the home to return, then use Refresh status. If pairing fails, open Connect a device on the Mac again and follow its current connection instructions.

Updates and recovery

Open Settings → Home → Check for updates to see whether your home or app needs an update. If an interrupted update offers Resume update, use it. For backup or local recovery, open Manage this home and backups… on the Mac and expand Advanced backup and recovery.

Contact during the beta

In TestFlight, open Home23 and choose Send Beta Feedback. You can also reply to the TestFlight invitation email. Describe what you were doing, which device you used, and what happened. Remove private conversation or household details from any screenshot before sending it.

For how messages, attachments and optional services move between your device and home, read Privacy.
