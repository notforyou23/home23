# The persistent resident

Home23's primary resident pays attention to a life, carries interests and responsibilities forward, acts within standing authority, and shares meaningful discoveries, progress, questions, good news and problems. Useful curiosity and changed understanding do not need a task. Repeated machine readings and fresh timestamps are not progress.

This is part of the existing resident, conversation and Work system. It does not introduce a second agent center or a message/task quota. The continuity direction in [Home updates and portability](HOME-UPDATES-AND-PORTABILITY.md) remains authoritative for software delivery and preservation of lived state.

## Attention and judgment

Discovery treats recurring observations as observations, including health and pressure data. They cannot themselves satisfy an opportunity for real conversation or remembered inquiry. Corroborated critical machine conditions retain priority; raw free RAM and unavailable pressure cannot claim that priority.

Conversation salience preserves attributed owner contact within the existing bounded context. The resident's own reports remain context rather than fresh owner conversation triggers. Background machine deliberation receives the owner context separately from sensor evidence, so corrections can change conduct without upgrading narrative into a verified fact.

The critic may keep a connection without an action or message. Optional owner outreach carries exact supplied references. Machine contact additionally needs a grounded consequence. Durable topic/condition judgment suppresses cosmetic rewrites and timestamp refreshes while allowing meaningful escalation, recovery, progress, decisions and contextual connections. Delivery retries retain their original content, judgment and identity. Unsupported legacy pending intents are preserved in a held directory with a reason.

## From a kept thought to a move

The critic can propose one optional `residentInitiative` with purpose `action`, `exploration` or `question`, scope `private_research`, `private_artifact` or `verification`, a concrete reason, bounded next move, stop condition and exact supplied evidence. This describes intent and grants no tool authority. Routine sampling, duplicated diagnostics and manufactured tasks do not qualify.

The engine's initiative driver uses the existing agency tick. Before accepting new proposals, it durably records a forward boundary over existing pursuits. Dormant history remains intact and cannot consume current initiative capacity merely by refreshing a clock. Generic `advance_one_step` records are never executed as instructions.

A responsibility identity depends on purpose, scope and meaningful supplied evidence; a cosmetic move or stopping-condition rewrite cannot create fresh work. The exact admitted request has its own immutable digest and identity for transport replay. New sourced material can open a new inquiry; the existing Work outcome path owns continuation of an admitted responsibility. Bounded recent initiative history is supplied to the critic.

When the selected evidence includes the current source observation, its trusted content also enters that identity. Sampling clocks cannot reopen attention. Collected health values retain their source uncertainty and existing interpretation; this boundary adds no health thresholds or instructions. Machine observations use validated condition bands, so changing raw free RAM cannot manufacture a changed responsibility.

Current editor and authority checks apply before effects. Withdrawn attention or authority cannot trigger a replay; a read-only canonical status receipt can still settle the saved admission. Unknown status remains unknown. Cancellation and other terminal executions stay terminal for the same responsibility.

A withdrawn pending proposal can settle locally when Core confirms no admission at the status read. This records local withdrawal, not executor cancellation or accomplishment, and releases capacity without resending the effect.

## Durable execution and followthrough

The authenticated local bridge forwards through the resident's signed connection. Core resolves the current primary resident and its existing owner conversation; callers cannot select an identity, channel, principal or grant. Public owner-message submission and group scheduled-turn boundaries remain unchanged.

Core saves the exact bounded intent, instruction, prepared context, manifest and Work binding before dispatch. The canonical origin is resident-authored and human-readable. Execution reuses normal Work, Attempt, Lease, recovery and Stop controls, with one unresolved initiative at a time and a bounded deadline over its actual child/review lineage.

Execution termination is not proof of a desired outcome. Action initiatives use existing inspected outcome reporting and bounded revisit. An owner Stop remains authoritative over late reports and followthrough. Explorations and questions retain execution state without a fictional owner assignment or agency task.

An outcome review inspects the result without requiring another owner message. If the original successful result was already delivered and inspection adds nothing meaningful, Core saves a private no-owner-update disposition. An action must first have an accepted complete assessment with evidence; failed or unresolved execution cannot silently claim closure. Ordinary owner-message answer rules remain unchanged.

Normal conversation and Work routes surface the result. Existing phone clients can consume the additive provenance fields and optional assignment state; source, committed contact, notification acceptance, device visibility and owner acceptance remain separate evidence.

## Configuration and delivery

The primary resident requires live agency and explicit `agency.initiative.enabled` configuration. Model alias and reasoning effort are selected from the current house catalog, then retained on the admitted Work. Chat and background Codex transport share known Responses reasoning capabilities; unsupported models do not silently claim to apply effort.

Existing homes need a reviewed configuration delta and forward boundary before activation. Software deployment preserves resident identity, history, credentials, encoder choice and integrations. It uses the ordinary verified product update route, with a concrete quiet Work/cron boundary and recovery evidence; source overlays and process labels are not acceptance.
